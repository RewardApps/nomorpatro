Nomor Patro

Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 September 2026 · Applies to the Android app tech.nomor.patro

Nomor Patro is a Nepali calendar. It does not have accounts, and it does not collect anything that identifies you.

Your data

You do not need an account to use this app. We never ask for or store your name, email or phone number.

Your settings

Your language, theme and notification choices stay on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Internet use

The calendar works offline. The internet is only used to load rashifal and to fetch calendar data for years published after your version of the app.

Improving the app

Anonymous information about which screens are used and where the app crashes is collected through Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics). This includes an advertising ID. It is used only to improve the app, never to identify you.

Google processes this data as our service provider. Their handling of it is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Notifications

Reminders are created on your phone for upcoming festivals. You can turn them off any time in Settings.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects nothing that identifies anyone, it holds no personal data about children either.

Deleting your data

Everything the app keeps lives on your own device, so uninstalling the app deletes it. If you would like the anonymous analytics associated with your device removed as well, write to us at the address below and we will ask Google to delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page and the date at the top is updated.

Contact

If you have a question about this policy, get in touch with Nomor Tech at REPLACE@nomor.tech.
